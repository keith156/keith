Studio Heritage & Philosophy

We don't merely decorate rooms. We design the backdrop to your life's finest moments.

Founded over two decades ago, KK Interiors began with an uncompromising vision: that interior spaces should elevate well-being, clarity, and connection. Our multidisciplinary studio bridges architectural rigor with artisanal decorative sensitivity.

Having orchestrated more than 500 luxury residences, penthouses, executive corporate offices, and boutique hospitality spaces, our team manages every nuance—from initial structural partition reviews to customized bespoke millwork, curated fine art procurement, and custom lighting scenes.

Architectural Methodology

A Seamless Journey from Vision to Reality

We bring complete structure and clarity to interior creation. Every milestone is transparently phased, engineered, and executed to perfection.

01

Discovery & Spatial Audit

We meet in person or on-site to explore lifestyle routines, spatial requirements, budget parameters, and architectural aspirations.

Phase: 1 - 2 Weeks
02

Schematic Design & 3D

We engineer optimal floor plans and photorealistic 3D visualization, allowing you to walk through lighting scenes, textures, and joinery.

Phase: 2 - 3 Weeks
03

Procurement & Millwork

Detailed technical blueprints are drafted for custom joinery. Stones, fixtures, and artisan furnishings are ordered with rigorous quality checks.

Phase: 3 - 6 Weeks
04

Turnkey Reveal & Styling

Our studio supervises contractor execution, furniture placement, art installation, and white-glove staging for a breathtaking reveal.

Phase: Final Handover

Our initial consultation can be held at our studio (13 Namagembe Close) or directly at your property. We discuss your spatial vision, functional needs, timeline, and aesthetic preferences. Yes, turnkey execution is one of our flagship offerings. We coordinate with trusted electrical, plumbing, masonry, and millwork craftsmen. We oversee site deliveries, verify technical drawings, conduct quality audits, and handle white-glove placement of all furniture.

A single-room or living salon transformation typically requires 4 to 8 weeks from schematic design to handover. A full villa or penthouse turnkey renovation generally ranges between 8 to 16 weeks depending on custom millwork orders and material imports.
